Accessibility Statement

These interactive lecture materials for ISE 5405 (Optimization I, Virginia Tech) are built as HTML with the goal of conforming to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

What is provided

  • Full keyboard operation: arrow keys navigate slides and reveals; all quizzes, checkpoints, and proof workshops are button-operated; most graphical widgets have keyboard-accessible input equivalents (number fields, sliders) whose results are reported in text.
  • Text alternatives: every chart, diagram, and interactive canvas carries a description, and key results (optimal corners, objective values, feedback) are always stated in adjacent text, announced via live regions.
  • Mathematics is rendered with MathJax including assistive MathML for screen readers.
  • A linearized reading order: press H in any deck for the scrollable handout view — the recommended mode for screen-reader reading and for browser text zoom.
  • Reduced-motion support (animations and auto-rotation disabled when requested by the OS), visible focus indicators, and an untimed "relaxed mode" in timed activities.

Known limitations

  • Some drag-based demonstrations (e.g., rotating the 3-D cube, dragging vectors) are supplementary; their content is fully described in text, but the drag interaction itself is pointer-based.
  • PDF handouts exported via print are untagged; the HTML pages are the accessible primary format.
  • The freehand annotation layer is inherently visual.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, contact the course instructor — accommodations and alternative formats will be provided promptly. Virginia Tech students may also contact Services for Students with Disabilities.
