      How to use Ink Pad

      Get started Open a PDF — it stays on your device, nothing uploads. Share a document by link: add ?pdf=https://… to this site’s address and the PDF opens right here for whoever taps it. Add &sign=1 (or tap Just sign a document) for a big-button signing mode — perfect for phones. Recent files reopen with one tap; Resume rebuilds your last arrangement. N adds a blank page, W jumps to your blank pages.
      Pen & ink P pen · colors + 4 highlighters + custom color wheel · ● cycles thickness · E cycles erasers (whole-stroke ◌ / precision ✂) · C clears the page · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo anything. Rest your palm freely — only the Pencil draws. Highlighters auto-straighten; hold Shift for straight lines.
      Tools Lasso ⬚ circles ink to move, resize, recolor, or delete it · ✍ Signature: draw it big once (even on your phone — save/load via file), place it anywhere, reuse with one tap · the yellow sticky-note tool leaves comments that Adobe & Preview show in their comment panes; comments that arrive with a PDF appear as yellow markers — tap to read · Text boxes (A−/A+ sizes, click existing text to edit) · shapes ▭ ○ ↗.
      Pages ☰ Pages opens the organizer: drag cards to reorder (touch: press & hold to lift), append more PDFs, insert blanks anywhere, remove pages, and ⟳ rotate any page (or all) in 90° steps — your ink rotates along. Annotations follow their pages.
      Math & graphs The ✋ Move tool (V) grabs any text box, equation, shape, or stroke and drags it — the lasso still handles multi-select, resize, and recolor. The text tool's √x̄ TeX button turns a text box into rendered LaTeX (live preview while you type) · the pen toolbar’s ∿ Plot button stamps f(x) with axes onto the page as ordinary ink — undo, lasso, or erase it like any stroke · ＋ Blank page adds one instantly (last-used paper); its ▾ — or press & hold Board — picks blank, lined, grid, or dot paper (the ruling exports with the PDF).
      View & find Two-finger pinch or +/−/0 zoom (Ctrl+scroll on laptop) · drag or scroll to pan when zoomed · M opens the page menu with thumbnails and text search.
      Present F goes fullscreen with auto-hiding controls · Esc or F exits · L laser pointer · T hides/shows the toolbar · all pens stay available while presenting.
      Save & export ⤓ Export PDF is the best export — your ink becomes part of a real PDF with selectable text and perfect quality. H print-style handout as backup. Annotations auto-save every stroke; ↓/↑ in the pen toolbar back them up to a file.
      What this app stores on your device: annotations, cached PDFs (for Recents & Resume), signatures you chose to remember, and preferences — all in this browser only, nothing online. Remove everything with the button below, or in your browser’s settings under site data for this site.
      Pages Drag cards to reorder (touch: press & hold to lift) — or use the ◀▶ buttons. To move several pages at once: click the first, then the last — the whole run between selects — then drag any selected card. Click a selected page to clear.
