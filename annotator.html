Ink Pad

Blank pages + annotate your own PDFs — same pen as the lectures
Your PDF stays on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

→/← pages · P pen · E eraser · W first blank page · N new page · H handout → save PDF · ? help

Pages

    Keyboard controls

    → / Space / click — next reveal or slide
    ← — back  ·  Home / End — first / last slide
    F — fullscreen  ·  M — slide menu / jump
    P — pen  ·  E — eraser (press again for precision mode ✂)  ·  C — clear slide
    H — handout view (then Ctrl/Cmd+P → PDF)  ·  L — laser pointer
    W — blank whiteboard pages, and back (pen auto-on)
    ? — toggle this help  ·  Esc — close overlays
    iPad / touch: an on-screen toolbar appears (pen, clear, arrows, handout, help). Annotations save automatically per slide and are included when you print / save a PDF. Pen toolbar: ↓ exports an annotations backup file, ↑ re-imports one (works across devices), ∅ erases everything.
