ISE 5405 · Practice Arcade

Games and drills for the first weeks of Optimization I. ← lectures
How credit works: each activity issues a completion code tied to your name when you hit the score threshold. Paste the code into the corresponding Canvas quiz. Codes are personal — they fail verification under anyone else's name.

Corner Hunt

A random LP appears — click the optimal corner before the clock runs out. Unbounded and infeasible rounds lurk. Trains: geometry, §1.4 & Ch. 2.

Formulation Forge

Assemble LP models from word problems: decisions, constraints, objective — with feedback on every choice. Trains: modeling, §1.2.

Proof Puzzles

Scrambled proofs — restore the logical order. Includes the √2 proof with the famous lemma. Trains: proof structure, Lectures 3–4.

Drill Deck

Flashcard drills on notation, standard-form conversions, and vocabulary. Missed cards recycle until mastered; progress saves. Trains: fluency.
Instructors: completion-code verifier. All tools are single HTML files — no accounts, no server; progress and names stay in the student's browser.
